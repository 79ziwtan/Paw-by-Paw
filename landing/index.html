The problem

Training isn't a sales pipeline. Trainers' tools think it is.

Training is specific to each behavior, often covers several dogs in one household, and doesn't move in a straight line. Generic CRMs are built for a linear sales funnel, so trainers work around them.

“Finding payment info, consult notes, or even a dog's name takes too many steps.”

— John Hayes, Dog Wizard

Grounded in our customer discovery: interviews with dog trainers, including Dog Wizard, a multi-location training business, plus pet owners, shelters, and rescues.

Dogs switch trainers mid-program

On a team, one dog can work with several trainers. If a dog is halfway through leash walking and its next lesson is with someone new, that trainer has to find out fast where things left off.

Dog history is scattered

It lives in trainers' heads, in Word and PDF files, and across separate tabs in tools that weren't made for training.

Progress is hard to see

Goals change mid-program and dogs regress. A pipeline stage can't show that a dog held a stay for 45 seconds at 20 feet near other dogs.

Owners get "report cards" they can't use

Hand-typed texts after each session don't tell owners what to practice, so progress stalls between sessions.

The solution

One structured record per dog,
from first consult to graduation.

Waffle models the way training actually works: flexible plans, behavior-by-behavior progress, and households with more than one dog. Everything else (scheduling, POS, payroll) stays in the tools you already use.

  1. Set up the household

    Owner, phone, payment status, and every dog with its own goal and safety flags.

  2. Build the plan

    Start from your own curriculum template, then reorder or rename stages for this dog.

  3. Log the session

    Tap behaviors, record the 3 Ds, dictate notes. About a minute, right from the field.

  4. Update the owner

    Send a no-login link with what you worked on and 2–3 homework tasks they can check off.

Who it's for

Made for trainers running
multi-session programs.

Primary customer

Independent trainers & small teams

Solo trainers and businesses with 1–10 trainers who coach dogs through packages of sessions. On a team, the same dog often works with more than one trainer.

  • Sell multi-session training packages
  • Work from their phone between visits
  • Hand dogs off between trainers mid-program
  • Juggle several dogs per household
  • Short on admin time; want logging done in the field
  • Already use separate tools for scheduling and payments
Also serves

Dog owners

The trainer's clients. They receive updates and homework through a simple link, with no app to download and no account to create.

  • Often less tech-savvy
  • Want to understand their dog, not just "fix" it

Core features

Three things that make Waffle different.

The at-a-glance dog card

Dog, owner, payment dot, goal, safety flags, current stage, and the last two sessions on one screen. Any trainer can pick up exactly where the last one left off.
